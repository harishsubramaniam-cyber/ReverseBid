A reverse auction runs the opposite way to a normal one: you publish the most you will pay, and suppliers compete by going lower. The lowest price wins.

{% endif %}

The words we use

Starting price
The most you will pay per unit. No bid can be above it.
Decrement
How much lower each new bid has to be than the current best.
L1, L2, L3
L1 is the lowest price right now — the winning position. L2 is second lowest.
Auto-extension
A bid in the last moments pushes the clock back, so nobody wins by bidding at the buzzer.
Baseline & savings
Baseline is quantity × starting price. Savings is baseline minus what you actually awarded.
